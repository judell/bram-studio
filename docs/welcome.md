Bram Studio is two things working together.

The **editor** open in your browser is where you'll do most of your work: cutting screencasts from recordings you've already made, and editing podcasts by their text.

This window is where an assistant can act on your computer. It can start Studio's server, install the optional pieces a feature needs, explain how anything works, and change whatever you'd like changed.

When something in the editor needs that kind of help, it will bring you here with a message ready to go. You can also just ask the assistant anything, right here.
